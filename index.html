Seat Monitor — new portal (/register/)

Read-only watcher for the redesigned portal. It opens a browser window: log in there yourself (solve any verification), go to Course Registration, and keep that window open. It watches the courses below and, when one changes from Full → seats available, it gives a loud desktop alert and focuses that course's Add button. It never clicks anything — you press Enter / click Add. Clicking Add adds the course; never click the final Submit (it locks your choices).

It keeps watching while minimised or in the background and refreshes the portal every ~30s, so it catches drops hands-free and alerts your laptop. Keep the portal window open (minimised is fine) and your laptop awake — if the lid closes and it sleeps, nothing runs until it wakes.
