Seat Monitor — new portal (/register/)

Course-seat watcher for the redesigned DTU registration portal. Open the portal, log in yourself, solve any verification, navigate to Course Registration, and keep the portal window open.

Add the courses you want to watch and start monitoring. The app checks these courses every few seconds. If a Full course shows at least 1 seat, it automatically clicks that course's + Add button and sends a normal desktop notification to confirm that the course was selected.

Monitoring supports one available course per slot. It skips courses already marked Added or Drop. It never clicks the final Submit button, so the final submission remains under your control.

Keep the portal window open and the laptop awake. Minimising or switching windows is fine. If the laptop sleeps or the portal page changes, monitoring will not run until it is active again.
