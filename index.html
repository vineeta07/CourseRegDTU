Seat Monitor — new portal (/register/)

Each panel below is one student login with its own portal window and its own cookies, so logins never mix. Press Start Monitoring, then log in yourself in the window that opens (its title shows the login name) and go to Course Registration.

The moment a listed course shows a seat, its + Add button is clicked in that login's portal. Only the courses listed for that login are touched. Courses marked Added or Drop are skipped, and the final Submit button is never clicked.

Portal windows can be hidden, minimised or covered. Keep the laptop awake.
